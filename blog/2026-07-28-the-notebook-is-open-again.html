Notebook Update • July 28, 2026 • Short read

The notebook is open again.

The site is finally easier to update, which means more stories, field notes, and regular posts from wherever the road happens to lead.

Summer is here, the website is finally updated, and this Notebook is ready to become active again.

For a long time, posting anything here required enough extra work that writing an entry could become its own project. By the time I finished formatting pages, updating links, preparing images, and making everything fit the rest of the site, the thought I wanted to share had sometimes already moved on.

That has finally changed. The new Notebook system is organized, searchable, easier to maintain, and simple enough that publishing a story no longer has to become an all-day technical problem.

A more regular rhythm

My goal is to post here twice a week. Some weeks may be busier than others, but I want to publish at least one update or story every week.

Twice a week is the goal. Once a week is the minimum.

Some posts will be current updates from camp, the road, a project, or whatever I am working through at the time. Others will be longer stories about places, people, and moments that shaped the life I am living now.

Notebook plan: Expect a mixture of current field notes, personal stories, project updates, photographs, technical rabbit holes, and older memories finally written down.

Not everything happened recently

A story does not have to be new to be worth telling. There are experiences from many years ago that I have never properly written about, along with photographs, memories, and unfinished thoughts that still connect to who I am now.

I may move backward through time as often as I move forward. One week could be about something that happened yesterday. The next could return to childhood, Florida, an old job, a road trip, a vehicle, a person, or a moment that did not make sense until years later.

This is not a record written in order. It is a place for the pieces that are finally ready to be told.

More than polished stories

Not every entry needs to become an essay. Some will be short updates. Some may be photographs with a few paragraphs. Others may become long stories, detailed project notes, or something I keep adding to over time.

The important part is that the Notebook is no longer sitting quietly while everything else keeps happening. Summer has arrived, the site is ready, and I have a lot of stories waiting for somewhere to go.

This is where they will start showing up.
