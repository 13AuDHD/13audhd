Accessibility

Accessibility Policy

The goal is a website that remains useful, understandable, and navigable across different devices, needs, and ways of interacting.

Last updated July 26, 2026

Commitment

I try to make 13audhd.com accessible to as many people as reasonably possible, including visitors who use keyboards, screen readers, zoom, high-contrast settings, reduced-motion settings, voice controls, or other assistive technologies.

Accessibility is an ongoing part of the website rather than a finished checklist. Pages, photographs, designs, and interactive features will continue to change, and accessibility problems may occasionally appear.

Device and browser preferences

The website may respond to accessibility and display settings enabled on your phone, tablet, computer, or browser.

For example, enabling Reduce Motion may remove or simplify parallax movement, card reveals, smooth transitions, and other visual effects. This is intentional and is designed to preserve the content while reducing nonessential movement.

Settings such as increased text size, page zoom, forced colors, contrast adjustments, content blockers, reader modes, reduced transparency, or reduced data may also change the appearance or behavior of parts of the site.

These settings may change the full visual experience, but they should not prevent access to the essential writing, photographs, links, and navigation.

Accessibility features

The site is being developed with features including semantic headings, keyboard-accessible links and buttons, visible focus states, image descriptions, responsive text, skip links, labeled navigation, and respect for reduced motion preferences.

Known limitations

Some older pages, image-heavy galleries, embedded third-party services, experimental interactions, or archived material may not yet provide the same level of accessibility as newer pages.

Third-party websites linked from this site are operated independently and may have different accessibility practices.

Report a problem

If any part of this website is difficult or impossible for you to use, please email 13audhd@gmail.com .

Please include the page address, what you were trying to do, the device or browser involved when known, and a brief description of the problem. You do not need to disclose a disability or diagnosis.

I will review the problem and figure out a reasonable way to correct it or provide the same information in another usable format.

Feedback

Accessibility feedback is welcome even when something is technically usable. If an interaction is confusing, exhausting, unpredictable, difficult to read, or harder than it needs to be, that is useful information.
