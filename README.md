## About me

I’m **Thirteen Nebula**, a designer, photographer, writer, traveler, and professional collector of projects based out of Durango, Colorado.

Most days happen somewhere between camp and a forest road, with **Horizon**, my black 2004 Chevrolet Suburban Z71, carrying my home, workshop, mobile studio, tools, camera gear, and whatever idea I am currently building.

I am autistic, have ADHD and Tourette’s, and have spent most of my life trying to build a version of life that actually fits me. A lot of what I create comes from that space. I photograph the places that make me slow down, write about life outside and neurodivergence, design brands and ideas I wish already existed, and build small tools and websites that help turn strange ideas into something real.
